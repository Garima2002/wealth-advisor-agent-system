## 2. Regulatory & Financial Advice Limitations

- **Not Financial or Investment Advice**: The analytical metrics, anomalies, and insights generated by this system are designed solely to assist qualified financial advisors and compliance analysts. They do not constitute formal investment advice, fiduciary counsel, legal opinions, or tax recommendations under SEC, FINRA, or FCA regulations.
- **Strict Non-Execution Safety Invariant**: The system **NEVER** initiates, automates, routes, or executes trades, asset purchases, transfers, account freezes, or financial transactions. All follow-ups remain strictly advisory and require human authorization.

## 3. Anomaly Detection Assumptions & Boundaries

- **Pattern Detection vs. Fraud Accusations**: All rules evaluate statistical or behavioral deviations. An anomaly flag indicates a pattern warranting human review; **it does not establish fraud, money laundering, or unauthorized activity**. Legitimate capital purchases, tax settlements, or annual tuition payments naturally trigger threshold rules.
- **Sample Size Preconditions**:
  - Category outlier detection requires a minimum sample size ($N \ge 3$) to prevent statistical distortion from small samples.
  - Monthly spending spikes require at least two distinct calendar months of transaction history to establish a baseline.
- **Portfolio Data Boundaries**:
  - Portfolio concentration and risk-profile mismatch rules are evaluated **only** when portfolio holding records are explicitly supplied.
  - If portfolio data is omitted, the system **never hallucinates or fabricates** portfolio findings.

## 4. Technical & Architectural Assumptions

- **Currency Standardization**: The system defaults to `USD` as the primary reporting currency. Multi-currency transactions without real-time exchange rates are flagged with data-quality warnings.
- **Storage Scalability**: The embedded SQLite engine provides lightweight, zero-configuration local ACID persistence suitable for local evaluation, edge agents, or single-process workloads. Enterprise multi-node Kubernetes deployments should substitute a PostgreSQL or Aurora adapter conforming to `StorageInterface`.
- **Zero Paid LLM Dependency**: The system is engineered to execute completely offline without requiring paid third-party LLM API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic). It achieves sub-100ms execution times and predictable outputs.
